# The market story, the journey file

## Purpose

The owner is not building a method for picking stocks or a forecast of returns. They are building a
foundation they own, a picture of how the market prices, what it pays and what it does not, where
losses hide and where the few extra points come from, written in their words and tested on their
own questions, so that every decision about the portfolio follows from a mechanism they understand
and not from advice, folklore, a feeling, or the advisor's judgement. Podgórna is the reason it
matters, a decision made on a feeling that still feels bad, and the next thirty years of decisions
are to be made the other way, from understood mechanisms, with the feeling put in its place.

The essence the whole work turns on, agreed on 12.10.2026. The index, IWDA, is not an unadjusted
default, it is the portfolio of the average dollar investor, the consensus weights for a holder
whose money, spending and future are in dollars and who owns nothing else. Holding it alone means
accepting every one of its priors as one's own, the dollar share, the quarter in ten names, the
lean to growth and high beta. The owner is not that holder, a life in złoty, flats already held, a
career as the largest asset, a drawdown of their own they can sit through. So the design has a
spine: the market mechanisms, which is the story; the owner's priors; IWDA's priors; and what the
other third must hold so that IWDA's priors plus the third come out as the owner's. What the third
adds is a lean, the adjustment from the average holder to this one, held for fit and not for
forecast, sized to the owner's three numbers, and the view that the cluster is inflated is kept as
a view that changes one thing only, nothing is added to the cluster.

The knowledge has three jobs. It is what makes holding possible, understanding why the typical
path sits below the mean, why a fall is not a signal, why a sick company is cheap for a reason and a
cheap dull one may stay cheap, is the only thing that keeps the owner from selling in the fall,
chasing the story or being tricked, and holding through is worth more than any alpha. It places the
owner in the picture, not an average investor but a researcher whose expertise is the largest asset
they hold, with a flat in Gdańsk, 19% tax and FIFO, one currency, a month a year for the portfolio
and thirty years, across the three things they hold, what they own and run, what they own and
manage, what they lend. And it is the constitution for what comes after, the design of the
portfolio, the pool rules, the agents that will do most of the running, and the yearly check, all of
which must be derivable from the story and must not contradict it.

The test of success is not a return. It is that the owner can explain every position from the
mechanism, that nothing is sold in a fall, that they refuse what they do not understand, that the
agents can run rules written in advance, and that in a year they reread the story and change only
what the evidence changed.

Three files make the work. The story itself, docs/investments/2026-10-26-market.md, written one
section at a time in the owner's voice. The journal, docs/investments.md, whose entry "October 2026:
The market story" holds one line per closed section and later the decision. And this file, the map,
the state and the plan, read at the start of every session together with the memory files and
updated at the end of every one. It replaced themes.md on 12.10.2026.

Six words, kept apart. The portfolio is the whole, all three thirds. The index is IWDA. The basket
is the twenty companies under the cap. The fund half is minimum volatility and equal weight. A lean
is not a holding but how any portfolio or basket differs from the market's weights, the index has
none, the fund half a small one, the basket a large one, and "tilt" is the same thing in jargon and
does not enter the story. A profile is what a group of companies with the same shared risks has in
common once the company-specific parts cancel, its distribution is a shape, the funds call it a
style and the papers a factor portfolio, and "kind" was dropped for it on 13.10.2026.

How to read it. Parts 1 to 5 at the start of every session, the map, where we stand, the owner's
picture, the plan, the destination. Parts 6 to 9 when needed, the evidence, the process, the parked
material, the log.

## 8. Parked material for the pool rules and the decision

- **The idea.** This third holds what constrained investors cannot, boring low-beta quality,
  neglected mid-caps, sound companies sold by forced sellers. Their constraints are the premium, not
  an inefficiency, benchmarks, mandates and leverage limits do not go away. It is the Podgórna
  method applied to companies, see docs/podgorna.md, a bid is a constraint and not an assessment,
  the room fills on a date, hold what we would buy. It replaces the June high-beta basket, high beta

- **The goal.** IWDA's return with two thirds of its drawdown and a beta near 0.8, later levered to

The other side, cut from investments.md on 10.10.2026, the argument for the companies third against
more index, for the decision:

- **Where the idea comes from.** Podgórna taught that a bid is a constraint and not an assessment.
  The room pays 5% for a 4% stream because the buyers in it are bound, by credit or by the coupon,
  and the rare one who counts real takes the discount. The question for the third is whether
  companies can be read the same way.
- **In stocks the constraint sits mostly in the holding.** A manager can buy the low-beta stock, he
  cannot hold it through three years behind his benchmark. A fund can want the small company, it
  cannot deploy in it. A mandate must sell the deleted name, the spun-off one, the downgraded one.
  Nobody is paid to read the dull report. The original bullet said the door is always open in
  stocks, which Constraints corrected, MSCI World holds no Polish company, so buying fails too.
- **Their constraints are the premium.** Benchmarks, mandates, leverage limits and attention do not
  go away, so what they leave cheap stays cheap, boring low-beta quality, neglected mid-caps, sound
  companies sold on a bad day by someone who must sell. Not an inefficiency, a price for a
  constraint others cannot escape.
- **Why a third of chosen companies and not more index.** The index holds the market's contract
  and needs nothing. The third holds contracts chosen and kept, the Podgórna method on companies,
  name the contract and its dates, buy from a constrained seller, hold through, check, move on when
  it is priced or broken. No alpha from information, one to two points if held through the droughts
  and about zero if not. Its size is set by what it does besides return, understanding twenty
  businesses is what makes holding all the equity possible, and the learning is a return named as
  such. Its failure costs a sixth of a point of the whole.
- **How it differs from the flat.** The discount is points a year, not a quarter of the price,
  because entry is nearly free. It falls with the index in a crash, beta near 0.8, where the rent
  does not. It gives what a flat cannot, twenty names, liquidity, scale to millions with no extra
  hours, no tenants, and an equity third outside the ten names at the top of the index. The premium
  is paid in decades to the one who did not sell in year eight.
- **What it replaces.** The June high-beta basket. High beta is not paid, and a basket of volatile
  names would sit in the same cluster as the index's top.

Two states, cut from investments.md on 10.10.2026, the holding logic behind the card and the exits:

- **A company has states, like the flat.** With a dated constraint still on it, carry plus the
  upside of the constraint lifting. Without one, carry alone, earnings at the market's rate. The
  price in the first state carries a discount for the constraint, and the gap closes when the state
  changes.
- **The constraints a company can be under.** A forced seller in its stock, a deletion, a mandate, a
  fund closing. Debt paid down on a schedule. A plant or a product ordered and not yet earning. A
  spin-off nobody has a reason to own yet. No coverage, so nobody is paid to notice. Each has a date,
  or it is not a constraint but a fault.
- **Choosing the contract is choosing the risk.** Which constraint, how certain its lifting, how far
  the date. A deleveraging on a schedule is a low-risk contract, a product launch a high-risk one.
  The card names which, so the twenty names are a chosen mix of risks and not an accident.
- **The check is a fact, not a forecast.** Did the debt fall, did the plant start, did the index add
  the name, did coverage begin. The two-line check reads the state, the business doing its part and
  the price the pool's problem, which is what lets the holding survive the fog.
- **The exit is the state change.** Priced when the pool has looked and the gap is gone, broken when
  the constraint turns out to be a fault, expired when the date passes with nothing. Then the
  proceeds move to a company in the first state again, the rotation.
