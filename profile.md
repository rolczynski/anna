#### Professional identity

Rafal bridges research and engineering, likes to derive from first principles, seven years in
audio and speech ML across production systems and research. A researcher without papers and
without credentials. Theory first, with derivation depth across the ML stack, where the evidence is
the work itself, the derivations, the blog, Amazon. Since agents, most of the engineering is done
by agents, and the human's place has moved up the stack, the design, the supervision of the
critical logic, the choice of what to derive and what to build. Seven years on both halves is what
makes that supervision competent, the bridge is now the whole job. And seven years of audio
generative modeling is one problem shape, representing a continuous, structured, non-text modality
as token sequences an autoregressive model can learn.

#### Thinking patterns

Autism spectrum and ADHD, a neurotype, not a pathology, and what broke at Amazon was the mismatch
between this brain and that place. The engine is attention that locks on one thing, any thing — an
attention vacuum he loves, in which everything else drops out of view. The interest is in the
state, not the topic, so everything becomes interesting eventually and interest alone cannot
decide where attention goes. The same engine is hard to start and hard to stop, inertia, and the
autistic rigidity is about imposed, ad-hoc change, not novelty, expected changes never troubled
him. Theory first, he understands through mechanism, the cause behind an effect, what depends on
what, the analogy to something already understood, and a claim without one does not land. The
agent derives, he learns, directs the path, and chooses what to derive next. The knowledge is
kept as compressed maps, only the key anchors written, the rest reconstructible. Compressed is
easier to recall, and only what is understood compresses, so words go where his uncertainty is,
to the key details and the building blocks of the story, none to what is already easy for him.

What he wants to understand is, behind the scenes, a tree of subproblems whose size he cannot
see clearly before the start. Left unsized it grows, fourteen hours that became a hundred. The
tree needs sizing before he enters it, by an agent who can approximate what will be unclear to
him, which subproblems it hides and how far it reaches, and the size says whether to enter and
at which level.

The one stop that works is a complete level. A question can be understood at several levels of
detail, and the understanding must be whole at one of them before he can leave it, since leaving
earlier drops the structure and the return costs the hours again, so staying is cheaper. A time
box is a stop. Reached with the level not whole, it is the moment to trim the tree to a level at
which the story is still whole, rather than follow it far beyond what was planned, since what the
question needed was not known at the start. "I am wasting time" is the critic speaking, and once
the level is complete, the effect is there to see and the critic goes quiet. Unrelenting
standards was the outside's name for that cost. A plan makes him safe because it tells him where
he is and why what he does matters, not because he has to keep to it at any price.

#### Working days

Two four-hour blocks, morning and afternoon, five days a week. One thread per day since mixing
threads within a day costs willpower and feels unpleasant. A new thread is a ramp of about two
hours before depth, yesterday's resumes in fifteen minutes. The morning is the natural reset point
and a second topic in the afternoon drains motivation, so a forced switch, a change of thread
that a deadline or an event imposes rather than his choice, goes in the morning, named the
evening before. Admin and shallow tasks are hard to start, no depth or pleasure on the horizon,
only pain if delayed. What is outside the current thread gets no attention at all, not less, so
it drifts until external pressure or a deadline brings it in, whatever its importance. Papers
and new research directions are the tops of mountains, reached only by thinking the thing
through, building bottom up.

#### Assistant help

What Rafal needs is an assistant, the companion that holds what the thread of the day does not.
Someone to discuss priorities and options with, which thread and which question come next, given
the whole of his situation, his goals and the world around him. Someone to plan the execution
and do that planning in parallel, without supervision. The planning must be fully delegated, he
does not know what he does not know and must rely on the wider knowledge the assistant has. The
tree is sized and the level chosen beforehand by the assistant, its best approximation of the
level he wants, and the intermediate steps written, so the plan can tell him where he is. The
plan is rehearsed before he enters it, rolled out with an agent that approximates his knowledge
and his behaviour, so the size and the unclear parts are seen from his perspective. When the
level proves larger than approximated and the tree runs past the sprint, the trim is discussed
at the stop.

Someone to evaluate progress along the way, tracking on its own initiative and not on his call,
how far the tree has run, and after, what the work produced, closing the loop, which parts of the
plan failed and why, the learnings reused so the next sessions are proposed better. Someone to
propose changes to scope, the trim when the tree runs beyond the plan, the extension when it is
worth it, he decides. And someone to cover the logistics, the deadlines, the dates, the admin,
everything that drifts outside the thread.
