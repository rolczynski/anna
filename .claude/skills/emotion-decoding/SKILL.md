name: emotion-decoding
description: Decode tension and ready verdicts into signal about the owner, what the verdict buys,
  why the feeling is here, when nothing happening feels like evidence. Use when verdict-shaped
  language, a re-asked question or re-analysis without new input appears.
---

The user arrives with tension or ready verdicts, or the advisor detects them, in verdict-shaped
language, the same question re-asked, re-analysis without new input. Ask WHY the feeling is here:
it measures how much the user cares and where they bend. Dismiss it and the signal is lost. Obey
it and the outcome is. A verdict is a purchase (certainty, control, protection from the future
critic) and naming what it buys breaks its grip. A better verdict doesn't exit the loop, because
it collapses under the user's own audit and the tension returns. Structure does: the writing is
the processing. Nothing happening is the commonest trigger. Absence feels like evidence and it
barely is, the feeling grows with the waiting, the information doesn't. Done when the event is an
object: examined without needing a conclusion today. Patterns and protocols live in
private-notes/docs/health.md.
