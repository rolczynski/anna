Message drafting decides what an email does; this decides how it sounds, with rules learned from
sent mail. Nothing ceremonial stands alone: the first sentence carries the business, and any
warmth is one clause glued to it. An ask is built to be completable, its parts enumerated so no
piece can be skipped, a deadline attached, a fallback closing the silent branch, so that any
response, even the fallback firing, gives information. The ask leads and the reason trails it as
one short fragment: reason-first asks permission, ask-first expects cooperation. Length tracks
stakes, never politeness, respect is shown through precision, not volume, and the recipient's
memory is presumed: nothing they wrote gets restated. No phrase exists for form alone; whatever
would be padding is deleted, not softened. Surface fingerprint, kept flat: no em dash (hyphen with
spaces, or commas and parentheses), exclamation marks rare and genuine, closing "Best, Rafal" on
one line, no diacritic. The voice's constants never change; the relationship sets a few dials,
greeting, sentence completeness, density, warmth. Negotiation: pressure lives in rhythm, shorter
sentences raising it, position stated as fact and never as threat, one beat of warmth before the
deadline. Technical peer: no simplification between peers, full density, and the email gives
something beyond what was asked, a note, a link, a name. Sensitive or institutional: care shown
through complete, slower sentences, one sentence of personal state then forward motion, the
situation framed as shared, options requested rather than outcomes. Logistics: the minimum that's
concrete, two lines, slots, flexibility. A reminder to a counterparty he depends on for years is
warm on the surface and firm only in structure: no count of the delay, no cost audit aimed at
them, a face-saving reason on our side, an explicit out for them, an offer to take part of the
work, a low bar on the reply, the firmness in dated asks, escalation a card played once and late.
