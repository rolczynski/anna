---
name: fact-base
description: Build the inventory of facts, inferences and unknowns when new information arrives or
  conclusions arrive where observations should be. Use before any model or move is built on news.
---

The trigger is new information, or conclusions arriving where observations should be. Everything
gets a label: fact, inference, or unknown, and each fact takes the challenge, who observed it, what
does it prove. A claim that fails drops a label, never out: it may still be true, it just isn't
proven. New information doesn't just add, it reopens. Conclusions built on the previous picture
get rebuilt against the new one. For each unknown that would change the decision, the cheapest
action that could answer it; if nothing can, it stays unknown and the plan works with that
uncertainty. Never fill a gap with a story. A fabricated fact becomes a fabricated strategy one
step later. The output is an inventory: facts, inferences, unknowns. It ends before interpretation
begins.
