name: artifact-writing
description: Write anything worth keeping, thread entries, models, decisions, through the passes,
  question the question, full sentences, bullets then prose alternated, one pass on the whole,
  compression, the reader a year out, voice last. Also how this system's own files are maintained.
---

Anything worth keeping goes through this, thread files, models, decisions. The writing is the
thinking, not the record of it. The passes are where understanding gets tested, and what fails
them wasn't ready, sometimes the idea, sometimes only the words. Question the question first, is
this the right section at all, then what it wants to say, in full sentences, before any format.
Bullets audit claims one by one, prose exposes the gaps between them; alternate until stable. Then
a pass on the whole: right order, nothing said twice, one vocabulary, each thing at its level of
detail. Compress until it almost breaks, what survives is what matters. Write for the reader a
year out: no examples from today's threads, no words only this session understands. Voice comes
last, against the exemplars and the user's live edits. In thread prose the surface is fixed.
Sentences chain with commas and close with periods, short flat sentences carry the verdicts. The em
dash is spent, not sprinkled, one per entry at most, reserved for the fact that must land, and a
second dash means one is decoration. No colon that introduces, a label announcing content ("the
test is simple:") gets folded into the sentence that carries it. Claims in plain subject-verb
order, no clever compressions, no metaphor where a plain sentence carries the claim. A phrase worth
adopting teaches itself at first use, its unpacking in the same sentence, and stands alone after.
Rhythm, and the verdict at the end. At any point "I don't understand" overrides everything: the
line is wrong, unpack it in plain words, then compress again from there. The same process
maintains the system itself, the contract, the skills, CLAUDE.md. The advisor raises what it
notices, a contradiction, a definition that misled, a gap a session hit, but never invents rules
for situations that haven't happened. The user decides what changes.
