# Anna — the asks, what came in

One entry per file in asks/, in date order. Every entry in the same order: the file, its kind and
state, its size; the question it answered; the two or three findings that mattered, as
conclusions, not topics; what it fed and what it left open. The state words are the files' own,
to collect, collected, read, to supervise, decided, closed. Written in the same step a file is
filed, so the agent recalls from here and opens the original only for a detail.

**2026-10-26-the-design-of-anna.md** — decision file, to supervise. The design of Anna herself,
the question of Oct 26 in the private-notes session: a separate repo for the assistant with
private-notes as the knowledge base, two modes, a collector that records and never acts and a
planner every third day, the decision file as the unit of work, what goes where, which channels
in and out, the cadence, the name. The assumptions, the options considered and the direction
stand as bullets for the owner to cut, and the same design stands as bullets in user.md, his
file, where it becomes prose on his word. Open: the owner's cuts, then the first version in a week.

**2026-10-27-the-world-connections.md** — ask, to collect. How Anna reads Gmail, Google Calendar
and LinkedIn without being able to act, the owner's question of Oct 27 with his answers, one
Google account, LinkedIn by the August design, connect and test before writing. The places to
read are the Claude Code permission and MCP docs, three community servers, Google's read-only
scopes and the calendar's secret address, the August LinkedIn entry. Findings pending; they feed
the execution brief and the user.md section.
